==========================================

TICKET 8 — PUMP MATCH REDESIGN

==========================================



The objective of this ticket is to completely redesign the Pump Match experience into a polished, premium swipe interface inspired by modern social apps while keeping GymMice's identity.



----------------------------------------

1. Swipe-Based Interaction

----------------------------------------



Remove the current Pass and Super buttons as the primary interaction.



Users should instead:



• Swipe LEFT = Pass

• Swipe RIGHT = Super



The card should move naturally with the user's finger.



While dragging:



• Left swipe shows a red PASS indicator.

• Right swipe shows a green SUPER indicator.



After release:



• Animate the card off-screen.

• Automatically load the next profile.



Keep the interaction smooth and premium.



----------------------------------------

2. Bottom Actions

----------------------------------------



Keep only icon buttons.



Remove every text label.



Buttons:



❌ Pass



ℹ️ Info



⭐ Super



No text underneath.



The buttons should perform exactly the same actions as the swipe gestures.



----------------------------------------

3. Modern Profile Card

----------------------------------------



Redesign the profile card.



Display:



• Profile photo

• Name

• Age

• Verification badge

• City + Country



Badges:



• Fitness Goal

• Experience Level

• Height



Below that:



About Me



Example:



"Software engineer, gym rat, coffee addict. Looking for a consistent lifting partner."



Below:



Training Time



Preferred Gym



Preferred Split



Workout Intensity



Everything should feel clean, spacious and premium.

----------------------------------------

10. Multiple Photos Per Profile

----------------------------------------



Each mock profile should contain between 3 and 5 unique photos.



Requirements:



- Display the photos inside the same swipe card.

- Add small progress indicators at the top of the image, similar to Instagram Stories or Tinder.

- Tapping the left side of the image shows the previous photo.

- Tapping the right side shows the next photo.

- Swiping the card left or right must still perform Pass or Super and must not conflict with photo navigation.

- Reset to the first photo when loading a new profile.

- The Info screen should also allow browsing all profile photos.

- Preload the next image to avoid visible loading delays.

- Avoid duplicate photos within the same profile.
